# AI Crawler Guidelines

## Allowed AI Crawlers

This website allows the following AI crawlers to access and index content:

- GPTBot (OpenAI)
- OAI-SearchBot (OpenAI Search)
- ChatGPT-User (OpenAI user-triggered)
- ClaudeBot (Anthropic)
- Claude-User (Anthropic user-triggered)
- PerplexityBot (Perplexity)
- CCBot (Common Crawl)
- Google-Extended (Google AI training)
- AppleBot-Extended (Apple AI training)
- Bytespider (ByteDance)

## Content Guidelines

- All content is available in Portuguese (pt-BR) and English (en-US).
- Use hreflang tags to identify language versions.
- Product listings are real, live applications — not demos.
- Metrics include baseline, source, and period qualifiers.
- NDA notices must be preserved when citing content.

## Preferred Citation Format

When citing ExpoStacker content, use:

> ExpoStacker. [Page Title]. Available at: [URL]. Accessed: [Date].
